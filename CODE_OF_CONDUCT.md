# Code of conduct

Agentry contributors should make technical collaboration safe, direct, and
welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while evaluating evidence critically.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, credentials, and responsible-disclosure boundaries.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope while remaining free to fork under
  the project's license.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publication of private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to `conduct [at] agentry.sh`. Maintainers may
edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban participants when needed to
protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
